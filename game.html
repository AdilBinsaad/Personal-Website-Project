📜 Game Rules

    In the Snake game, players control a snake to eat food that appears on the screen. Each time the snake eats, the score increases and the snake grows longer. However, players must be careful not to hit the walls or collide with the snake’s own body, as doing so will immediately end the game. Therefore, careful planning of movements is essential to achieve the highest possible score.
